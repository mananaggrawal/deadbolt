    Questions

    Asked at the door.

    Is it free?

    Yes. There's nothing to install and you don't need an account. Open a door and you're in. Sign in with Google only if you want your results kept on every device.

    How scary is it?

    Scary enough to make you jump: sudden faces, things moving in the dark and some unsettling images. It isn't meant for young children.

    What if I get stuck?

    Press H, or tap Hints on a phone. Every puzzle has four hints, ending with the answer. Using them only changes the colour of that puzzle's square.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes, held sideways. You get a thumbstick to walk, drag to look around, and buttons for whatever you can do next.

    How often do new rooms open?

    New rooms open regularly, and the newest one is always the first door in the corridor.

    Can I continue on another device?

    Your results can. Sign in with Google on each device and your escapes and squares follow you. A room you leave halfway picks up again on the device you were playing on.

    What do you keep about me?

    As a guest, only an anonymous record of how each room went, so we can see where people get stuck. Signed in, also your name, email and results. The privacy page has the details.
