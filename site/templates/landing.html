    Before you go in

    What you need.

    • A screenA computer with a keyboard and mouse, or a phone held sideways.
    • SoundHeadphones. Most of what happens in the dark, you hear before you see.
    • Time20 to 40 minutes. Leave halfway and the room waits for you on this device.
    • CompanyNone. Every room is played alone.

    Questions

    Asked at the door.

    Is it free?

    Yes. There's nothing to install. You sign in with Google once, then open a door and you're in. Your escapes and squares are kept on every device you sign in on.

    How scary is it?

    Scary enough to make you jump: sudden faces, things moving in the dark and some unsettling images. It isn't meant for young children.

    What if I get stuck?

    Press H, or tap Hints on a phone. The hints go from a nudge to the answer itself. Using them only changes the colour of that puzzle's square.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes, held sideways. You get a thumbstick to walk, drag to look around, and buttons for whatever you can do next.

    How often do new rooms open?

    New rooms open regularly, and the newest one is always the first open door in the corridor.

    Can I continue on another device?

    Your results can. Sign in with Google on each device and your escapes and squares follow you. A room you leave halfway picks up again on the device you were playing on.

    What do you keep about me?

    Your name, email address and results, and a record of how each room went, so we can see where people get stuck. The privacy page has the details.
